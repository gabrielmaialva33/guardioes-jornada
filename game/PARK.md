# Secret Trail: the park ("Parque da Iara")

The hand-made park is the Reserva da Iara (`LEVEL.md`, Act 1) plus four hand-made zones around it (Acts 2 and 3), with the endless generated world beyond (`WORLD.md`).

- **Plan image:** `docs/park-plan.png`, generated by `docs/park-plan.py`. That script holds the coordinates and is the source for this file.
- **Approval:** the owner approved the plan on 26/10/2026.
- **Units:** game units. The avatar is 3 u tall, 1 u ≈ 0.47 m, walking is 9.6 u/s, and a full jump is 5.2 u on the flat (`jumpReach`). x grows east (the serra), z grows south.

## 1. Why

- **Size:** today the Reserva is 160 × 160 u (about 75 m across). The main loop is 40 s of walking and the whole story takes about 10 minutes.
- **Target:** the park is 360 × 480 u (about 6× the area), with **40–60 minutes** of hand-made play before the endless world.
- **Densify, don't just enlarge:** at most ~300 u (30 s) along a trail without something notable (WORLD.md §4). Each zone has 4–5 missions, at least 1 secret, 1 landmark visible above the fog, and 1 new mechanic.

## 7. Guia da Mata v1 (the field guide)

- **Entries:** one per species. Each has the pt-BR common name, the scientific name, habitat, what it eats, status (for example "ameaçado") and one sourced fact.
- **How entries are earned:** by photographing the species, or by completing the mission that features it.
- **Unknown entries:** shown as a silhouette, Pokémon-style.
- **Rarity and time:** each species carries `rarity` and `time` (day, dawn or dusk). The endless world will use these later.
- **Saving:** entries are saved per device in `gameSave`.

The UI is a new panel opened from a HUD button. It is the seed of the "pegada Pokémon" loop the owner wants for later.

## 8. Budgets and streaming

- **Loading:** zones load through the F1 chunk streaming (Codex). Until that lands, each zone can be loaded alone as a test map (`?debug&zone=vale`) through the same `SurvivalWorld` shape the Reserva uses.
- **Per-zone placement cap:** like the Reserva, ≤ 1,600 trees, rocks and decorations.
- **Frame gates:** the low-tier gates of ART.md apply to every pose of every zone: ≤ 150 draws, ≤ 200k tris and p95 ≤ 33 ms on the A07.
- **New landmarks:** the Cachoeira Grande, the tower and Vovó Jequitibá get fog-free silhouettes, as the jequitibá and the ipê already do.

## 10. What we take from Green Hell: knowledge, not files

The owner owns Green Hell and extracted it for local study (`tmp/greenhell-port/`, local-only rules in `ART.md` §12). Nothing from it ships. What we do take is what we **learn** from it, measured and then rebuilt in our own style:
